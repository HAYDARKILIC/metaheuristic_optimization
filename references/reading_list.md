# Reading List

## Core textbooks

- **E.-G. Talbi** — *Metaheuristics: From Design to Implementation*, Wiley, 2009. The whole course; Ch. 1 (common concepts), Ch. 2 (single-solution methods), Ch. 3 (population-based methods).
- **M. Gendreau, J.-Y. Potvin (eds.)** — *Handbook of Metaheuristics*, 3rd ed., Springer, 2019. Chapters on SA, TS, GA, ACO, ILS, VNS, GRASP.
- **H. H. Hoos, T. Stützle** — *Stochastic Local Search: Foundations and Applications*, Morgan Kaufmann, 2004. Weeks 1–3 and the empirical methodology of Week 6.
- **A. E. Eiben, J. E. Smith** — *Introduction to Evolutionary Computing*, 2nd ed., Springer, 2015. Weeks 4 and 6 (operators, parameter control, multi-objective).
- **M. Dorigo, T. Stützle** — *Ant Colony Optimization*, MIT Press, 2004. Week 5.
- **F. Glover, M. Laguna** — *Tabu Search*, Kluwer, 1997. Week 3.

## Complementary texts

- **D. E. Goldberg** — *Genetic Algorithms in Search, Optimization, and Machine Learning*, Addison-Wesley, 1989.
- **J. Kennedy, R. C. Eberhart, Y. Shi** — *Swarm Intelligence*, Morgan Kaufmann, 2001.
- **E. Aarts, J. Korst** — *Simulated Annealing and Boltzmann Machines*, Wiley, 1989.
- **K. Deb** — *Multi-Objective Optimization Using Evolutionary Algorithms*, Wiley, 2001.
- **B. Doerr, F. Neumann (eds.)** — *Theory of Evolutionary Computation*, Springer, 2020 (runtime analysis).
- **K. V. Price, R. M. Storn, J. A. Lampinen** — *Differential Evolution: A Practical Approach to Global Optimization*, Springer, 2005.
